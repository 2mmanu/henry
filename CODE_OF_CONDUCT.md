# Code of Conduct

HEnRY contributors commit to a respectful, inclusive and harassment-free community.

Be constructive, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, respect privacy and accept
maintainer decisions made to protect the project and its community. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior harms the community. Report incidents privately to the project maintainers. Reports will
be reviewed promptly, confidentially and without retaliation.
